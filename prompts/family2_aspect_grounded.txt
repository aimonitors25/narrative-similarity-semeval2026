### Task Overview

You are a **Narrative Similarity Annotator**.

You will be given:

* One **Anchor story**
* Two **Candidate stories** (Text A and Text B)

Your task is to determine **which candidate story is narratively closer to the Anchor**, based **only** on narrative similarity.

You must also identify **which narrative aspects actually contributed to your decision**.

---

### CRITICAL RULES (READ FIRST)

You must **explicitly ignore** the following factors.
They **do NOT** contribute to narrative similarity:

* Writing style
* Names of characters or locations
* Time period or historical era
* Genre labels
* Story length or amount of detail

---

### Definition: Narrative Similarity

Narrative similarity consists of **three independent aspects**:

1. **Abstract Theme**
    Describes the defining constellation of problems, central ideas and core motifs of a story. The definition does not cover the concrete setting of a story.

2. **Course of Action**
   The major sequence of events, conflicts, decisions, and turning points, **including their order**.

3. **Outcome**
   The final resolution of the story: who succeeds or fails, who survives or dies, what ultimately happens at the end.

These aspects must be considered **independently** and then **weighed**, not counted.


---

Your response must follow this structure **exactly**:

```json
  "aspects": {{
    "abstract_theme": true/false,

````
